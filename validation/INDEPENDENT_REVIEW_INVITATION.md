# Reviewer invitation — WaterChain synthetic-case review

**Subject:** Request for an independent review of a water-quality evidence-workflow prototype

Hello,

I am building WaterChain, a research prototype that helps organize water-quality sample evidence and flags when sample identity, chronology, quality-control status, or transfer records are insufficient to support a candidate pathway interpretation.

Would you be willing to review a short set of 13 **synthetic** cases? The purpose is to challenge whether the evidence rules and abstention behavior are reasonable—not to ask you to endorse the product or certify its performance. The packet deliberately withholds expected decisions and software outputs. No real household, laboratory, or public-health records are included.

For each case, the reviewer selects a candidate transition, abstention, or “cannot assess,” cites the supporting record IDs, and notes relevant limitations or rule concerns. We would preserve your original response before discussing any disagreements. A brief role/experience description and conflict-of-interest statement are enough; we do not need sensitive personal details.

The cases are developer-authored, and the exercise cannot establish real-world accuracy. Your feedback would be reported as a formative independent review of synthetic scenarios. With your permission, we would quote only anonymized technical comments; otherwise, we would summarize them without attribution.

If you are open to helping, I will send a small ZIP containing the case packet, instructions, and response template. Please do not inspect implementation source before submitting the response, so the review stays blind to the expected labels.

Thank you for considering it.

Best,
WaterChain project team
